Try the lifetime rules

The counter creates createScope<number | null>() and applies it with .pipe(scope). Unmounting calls its cleanup function.

  1. Switch the speed. The previous timer closes and the selected timer starts at zero.
  2. Choose the same speed again. The current connection keeps running.
  3. Pause. The timer closes; the view remains mounted.
  4. Unmount. The timer and the view’s three button listeners close together.
  5. Mount again. A new view receives a fresh scope.
